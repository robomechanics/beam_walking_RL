# Per-period speed x duty-factor surfaces (gait specialists)

Rendered from the period-sweep grids, one panel per commanded period. Solid markers are cells passing the frozen compliance and finite-energy gates; the faint plane behind them is the median over every non-failed trial with finite cost of transport, gates ignored. Duty anchors shown per gait are those feasible at that period: trot needs five swing control ticks, walk two.
