## Push-study source data

`push_study_conditions.csv` is computed from the local evaluation archives
(`results/validation_tight3_{nominal,perturbed}_20260913` for the narrow-stance
policy and `results/validation_tight3perturb_{nominal,perturbed}_20260913` for
the push-trained policy; 12 conditions x 64 trials each). Per condition it lists
success/failure/timeout counts, the success rate with a Wilson interval, and the
realized duty factor (fraction of settled samples with foot force above 5 N),
realized stance width (mean stance-phase left-right foot separation in the body
frame, averaged over the front and rear pairs), lateral RMSE, forward speed,
mean failure time, and the mean push envelope at failure. The realized duty
factor and width reproduce the audited evaluator's `achieved_df` and
`body_achieved_width` on the seed-2 validation archives to within 0.001.

Two properties of the fine-tuned checkpoints must accompany any use of these
numbers:

- Both checkpoints realize duty factors of about 0.67 / 0.72 / 0.78 for
  commands 0.50 / 0.625 / 0.75; the duty-factor contrast is compressed.
- The push-trained checkpoint realizes stance widths of 0.13--0.18 m for
  commands of 0.05--0.10 m; it does not test narrow-stance locomotion.

Pass `--from-csv` to regenerate the figures on a machine without `results/`.
